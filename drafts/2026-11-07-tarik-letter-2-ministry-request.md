# Лист 2 Таріку: запит на офіційний лист по ЖД для міністерства

Дата: 07.11.2026
Статус: драфт для відправки. Перевірити латинське написання назви компанії «Мірничий» (нижче Mirnychyi).

**Subject:** New opening for the railway pilot – need a short official letter from your side

Dear Tarik,

Hello again. I had quite an interesting conversation today and want to share it right away, because it changes what I wrote to you about the railway.

One of my acquaintances introduced me to people at the Ministry. There is a real possibility to raise our railway question almost at the top: there are several Deputy Ministers there, and the information can be presented directly in Kyiv. This is a different route from the regional railway management and from the Ukrzaliznytsia board, and it is the level where a decision like this can actually be taken.

What I was told is this: the question has to be formulated as a complete, formal request specifically for the railway. Not a technical note, but a short official letter that explains who is asking, what is proposed, and above all what the country and the railway get out of it. So I am asking you to prepare it from your side, and I will add my part and hand it over to the right people.

**What the letter should contain.**

1. **Who we are.** A short paragraph on Deutsche Infrasoft and your group: profile, railway references in Germany and the EU, your Deutsche Bahn background. A short paragraph on my company, Mirnychyi, as the Ukrainian partner responsible for field work, geodetic control and local coordination.
2. **What we want to do.** The pilot: a railway corridor of about 100 km in Western Ukraine, mobile laser scanning with high-resolution 360° imagery, GNSS-denied positioning methodology (INS, odometric aiding, ground control points), producing a georeferenced point cloud and a BIM model of the track and its infrastructure.
3. **What we hand over.** List the deliverables explicitly: the point cloud, the BIM model, the asset inventory, the imagery, the methodology document and the training of a Ukrainian team. State clearly that all results are transferred to Ukrzaliznytsia / the Ministry and remain their property.
4. **What they can do with it.** This is the most important part, so please write the maximum here: condition assessment and damage detection, planning of repairs and reconstruction, a digital base for EU-aligned (TSI) documentation, a ready methodology they can repeat on any line, a trained local team, and a reference case that supports funding applications to the EU and international financial institutions. Describe the future: how a BIM-based railway digital twin is used in Germany and what it will mean for Ukraine's reconstruction.
5. **Benefits for them, not for us.** Make it clear that the pilot is delivered as a volunteer / pro bono project, or whatever you prefer to call it, that is, your company is ready to invest its own resources and equipment into this pilot for the sake of a common future, and that the complete case will be handed over to the Ukrainian side. They need to see that there is a serious company willing to invest, not someone asking for a contract.
6. **Data security.** One short paragraph: where the data is processed and stored, who has access, that nothing is published or taken outside the agreed scope, and that we are ready to work under any security procedure they define. Without this paragraph the letter will not move.
7. **What we ask for.** A simple ask: approval in principle for the pilot, a counterpart on the railway side, and a selected corridor.

I think you know better than me what else should be included from your side, so please form it as you see fit, but keep it short: one to two pages, formal tone, on your company letterhead with your official signature. The signature from my side will be from Mirnychyi, and I will pass the letter to the relevant people.

Please do not drop the road option with the Lviv regional authority in the meantime. The two routes do not compete. If the Ministry supports the railway request, it also makes the road pilot easier, and if the railway takes time, the road pilot gives us the first Ukrainian result and the trained team anyway.

It would be good to have the letter within the next week or so, while the contact is fresh and the door is open. If it helps, send me a draft first and I will check it against what I was told about the format and the priorities.

Best regards,
Ievgen (Eugene) Lavrishko
Mirnychyi / GIS Point, Lviv

## Додаток: каркас спільного листа-запиту (для Таріка, щоб було простіше)

1. Шапка: бланк Deutsche Infrasoft, дата, адресат «Deputy Minister ... / Ministry of ...» (адресата уточнити після розмови з контактом).
2. Абзац 1: хто ми (Deutsche Infrasoft + Mirnychyi), одне речення про досвід і одне про мету співпраці з Україною.
3. Абзац 2: що пропонуємо (пілот ~100 км, технологія, результати).
4. Абзац 3: цінність для УЗ і держави (стан інфраструктури, планування відбудови, стандарти ЄС/TSI, навчена команда, основа для заявок на фінансування).
5. Абзац 4: умови (волонтерський/pro bono пілот, усі результати передаються українській стороні, безпекові процедури за вимогами замовника).
6. Абзац 5: запит (погодження в принципі, контактна особа, вибір ділянки) і готовність презентувати в Києві.
7. Два підписи: Deutsche Infrasoft (Tarik Ilyas) і Mirnychyi (Ievgen Lavrishko).
