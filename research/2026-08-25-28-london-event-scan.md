# London Trip Event Scan — 25–28 August 2026 (ICP-matched)

Research of exhibitions and conferences for a London stay **25–28 August 2026**, matched against GIS-Point ICPs (Unit 1 GIS/Survey & AEC · Unit 2 IT-GEO, AgriTech, Energy/Utilities · Defence warm-intro only). Compiled 21 Aug 2026.

## Verdict

**London has no ICP-relevant exhibition or conference on 25–28 August 2026.** Late August is the UK's B2B dead season: the ExCeL and Olympia calendars show nothing business-facing that week (ExCeL's only late-August booking is a consumer fan convention on 22–23 Aug), and the Monday 31 August bank holiday means many decision-makers are on leave. Treat the trip as a meetings week and plan a return for the late-September event cluster.

## How to play it

- **Play A — make August a meetings week (Tue 25 – Fri 28 Aug).** Pre-book 1:1s with signed and warm UK accounts (GCI, NP Survey), pipeline prospects from the IT-unit tier list, and partner conversations. Front-load Tue–Thu — Friday 28 empties early before the bank-holiday weekend.
- **Play B — return for the September cluster (Tue 22 – Wed 30 Sep).** World Agri-Tech (22–23) hits T2 Agro; DroneX + International Security Expo at ExCeL (29–30) hit survey/drone and defence-warm audiences; UK Construction Week Birmingham (29 Sep–1 Oct) covers AEC/Scan-to-BIM. Use the August meetings to set up September walk-throughs.
