# Event Scan — 25–27 September 2026 (ICP-matched)

Research of exhibitions and conferences around **25–27 September 2026**, matched against GIS-Point ICPs (Unit 1 GIS/Survey & AEC · Unit 2 IT-GEO, AgriTech, Energy/Utilities · Defence warm-intro only) in USA / Canada / DACH / UK. Compiled 21 Aug 2026.

## Verdict

**25–27 September 2026 is Friday–Sunday. No major ICP trade fair runs inside the window — and in London specifically, the ExCeL and Olympia calendars show nothing B2B that weekend.** London's ICP action sits either side of it: World Agri-Tech on 22–23 Sep, then DroneX + International Security Expo at ExCeL on 29–30 Sep (UK Construction Week Birmingham 29 Sep–1 Oct). Elsewhere, four relevant fairs (Germany) end on Friday the 25th.

## Two ways to play it (London base)

- **Play A — extend forward to DroneX (Fri 25 → Wed 30).** Stay through 29–30 for DroneX + International Security Expo at ExCeL (survey/drone, geo-SaaS, defence-warm audiences); UK Construction Week Birmingham (29 Sep–1 Oct) is a 90-minute train for AEC/Scan-to-BIM buyers. Fill Fri 25 with pre-booked London client meetings; the weekend is prep and outreach.
- **Play B — arrive early for World Agri-Tech (Tue 22 → Sun 27).** World Agri-Tech Innovation Summit (22–23, InterContinental O2) hits the T2 Agro/AgriTech ICP exactly. Thu–Fri 24–25 for follow-up meetings booked on the spot. Combined with Play A, one trip covers three ICP segments.

INTERGEO Munich (15–17 Sep) remains the single best-fit event of the month and is worth its own trip even though it falls outside this window.
