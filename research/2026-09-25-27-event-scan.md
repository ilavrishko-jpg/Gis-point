# Event Scan — 25–27 September 2026 (ICP-matched)

Research of exhibitions and conferences around **25–27 September 2026**, matched against GIS-Point ICPs (Unit 1 GIS/Survey & AEC · Unit 2 IT-GEO, AgriTech, Energy/Utilities · Defence warm-intro only) in USA / Canada / DACH / UK. Compiled 21 Aug 2026.

## Verdict

**25–27 September 2026 is Friday–Sunday. No major ICP trade fair runs inside the window.** Four relevant fairs end on Friday the 25th (last-day traffic only); nothing B2B runs on the weekend. The strong ICP events cluster on 15–17, 22–25 and 29–30 September.

## Two ways to play it

- **Play A — keep the dates, go Germany (Thu 24 → Fri 25).** Work Friday 25 at WindEnergy Hamburg (Energy/Utilities leads) or InnoTrans (infrastructure asset owners). It's a closing day — pre-book meetings instead of relying on walk-ups. The weekend has no B2B value.
- **Play B — shift 3–4 days, go London (Tue 22 → Wed 30).** World Agri-Tech (22–23) hits T2 Agro; DroneX + International Security Expo (29–30) hit survey/drone and defence-warm audiences; UK Construction Week Birmingham (29 Sep–1 Oct) covers AEC. One trip, three ICP segments. Strongest ROI if the dates can move.

INTERGEO Munich (15–17 Sep) remains the single best-fit event of the month and is worth its own trip even though it falls outside this window.
