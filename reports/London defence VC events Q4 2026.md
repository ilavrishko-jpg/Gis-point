# Book the first October week now

The best chance in Q4 2026 for a pre-seed defence founder to meet defence investors in London is **1–8 October 2026**, and that window opens six days after this report's research date (25 Sep 2026). London Defence Tech Week runs across those days. It contains the **Resilience Conference (5–6 Oct)** with its LAUNCH@RC company showcase, the **EDTH Defense Tech Days** with a VC & Founders Networking Night and a hackathon, a **Seedcamp + Singular session (6 Oct)** and the **Deep Tech London Summit (8 Oct)**. These are the only Q4 London events where the sources confirm named defence or dual-use investors: Twin Track Ventures, Linse Capital, 201 Ventures, Seedcamp and Singular, with NATO Innovation Fund and Amadeus Capital named as having given input to the programme. After 11 October the picture thins sharply. The one confirmed defence-investor event in London is the **LDC Investment Forum on 2 December**, and it is an institutional policy-and-finance gathering, not a pre-seed pitch venue. November offers general deep-tech angel exposure (UKBAA/SETsquared Investment Futures, 24 Nov), but no named defence investors. **Read every fact here with one caveat in mind:** the network proxy blocked nearly every official event site, so most dates, prices and names come from search-engine snippets of official or organiser pages, not from full page reads. Re-check them on the live pages before spending money or travel time.

**How to read the verification labels.** VERIFIED means a 2026-specific date or fact appeared in a snippet from the official or organiser page, or the organiser's own news post or X account. It does not mean the full page was read. PARTLY VERIFIED means the date is confirmed but sub-details (sub-event date, venue, investor list) are not. UNCONFIRMED means the information comes from an aggregator or third-party site, is not clearly about 2026, or is an inference. None of the official pages for eurodefense.tech, resilienceconference.io, londondefenceconference.com, deeptech.london, summit.sifted.eu, plexal.com or gov.uk could be loaded directly.

The Resilience Conference ranks first because it is the only event where the sources name three specialist defence investors as speakers ([Resilience Media](https://resiliencemedia.co/179449784-2/)). Its **LAUNCH@RC** track on 5 October is billed as "a unique opportunity to meet 20 of the most exciting companies in the sector" ([Resilience Media](https://resiliencemedia.co/something-new-something-big-at-resilience-conference-london/)). The catch is that the 20 companies have probably been chosen already. Even so, a ticket gets a founder into a small room with Twin Track, Linse and 201 Ventures. Eric Slesinger's role as founder of the European Defense Investor Network makes him a useful connector to the rest of the European defence-VC community ([Resilience Media](https://resiliencemedia.co/179449784-2/)). The sources disagree slightly on titles: one calls Bastiaan Janmaat a GP and another calls him a Partner at Linse Capital. The EDTH programme ranks second because its **VC & Founders Networking Night** is the only event in the window designed specifically to put defence founders and VCs together ([Resilience Media](https://resiliencemedia.co/the-london-defence-tech-week-programme-is-here/)). The date and venue of that night could not be found, and that gap needs closing within days. EDTH also ran a separate London hackathon on 26–28 June 2026, so do not mistake that summer edition for the October one ([Luma](https://luma.com/edth-2026-summer-london)).

**The urgency is real.** London Defence Tech Week starts on **1 October**, the EDTH hackathon is reported for **2–4 October**, LAUNCH@RC is on **5 October**, and the Deep Tech London Summit on **8 October** is already inside its no-refund window ([Luma](https://luma.com/deeptechldn26)). The organiser says some events "are open to register, others require an application, and some are invitation only" ([Resilience Media](https://resiliencemedia.co/the-london-defence-tech-week-programme-is-here/)). The application-gated ones are the most likely to close first. A founder who wants any of this should contact Resilience Media and EDTH this week, register for the Deep Tech London Summit at £100 ([deeptech.london](https://www.deeptech.london/)), and use the week to book follow-up meetings for November, when London offers little dedicated defence programming.

Note too what the LDC Investment Forum is not. The London Defence Conference calls it "the annual winter gathering" in the City, themed "Funding Our Future Defence" ([London Defence Conference on X](https://x.com/LondonDefConf/status/2095460146528805295)). Its audience of policymakers, military, investors and industry points to large funds and institutions, not angels writing pre-seed cheques. It is the right place to be visible to NSSIF-type co-investors and later-stage funds. Do not expect to close a first round there.

## Conclusion

The honest answer is narrower than the question implies. The evidence behind this conclusion is thinner than it looks, and that should shape what a founder does next.
