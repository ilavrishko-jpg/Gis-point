## Evidence discipline
Every number is tagged: **`[P]`** primary source · **`[S]`** secondary syndicated research (vendor
estimate) · **`[M]`** founder model/assumption. Claims refuted in adversarial fact-checking (loitering-
munition and "GNSS-independent PNT constellation" market sizes) are listed in Appendix B — do not use them.

## Headline findings
- **Problem:** ~10,000 drones/mo lost to jamming; 60–80% of FPVs miss under EW `[P]`.
- **Market:** Assured PNT $846M→$10.2B by 2035 (28.4% CAGR), military ~46% `[S]`; bottom-up attach TAM ~$600M/yr base `[M]`.
- **Competition is funded:** Auterion $130M, Advanced Navigation $110M, ANELLO $25M — mostly inertial/gyro, leaving the camera-primary absolute-fix lane open `[P]`.
- **Ask:** $750K SAFE (~$5–6M cap) + $0.5–1M non-dilutive → TRL 6–7 + field proof → $3–5M seed.

_Research date: July 2026. Funding/contract data is recent and will age quickly._
