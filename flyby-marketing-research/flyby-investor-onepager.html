01 The problem

GNSS is the single point of failure in drone warfare

In contested airspace, satellite navigation is jammed (signal denied) or spoofed (drone silently walked off-course). GPS-denial is now the default battlefield condition — and the front is already reaching for optical navigation to survive it.

  • ~10,000 drones/month lost, mainly to Russian electronic jamming P RUSI '23
  • 60–80% of FPVs miss their target on parts of the front P RUSI '25
  • Ukraine fielding neural-net optical nav since Dec 2024 P IEEE
02 The solution & edge

Camera-as-GPS, on a georeferenced base map

VIO dead-reckons from camera + IMU but drifts without bound; FlyBy's core layer matches live imagery to a georeferenced map to reset drift and output absolute lat/long.

  • Map-matching pipeline — GIS-Point's core competence; hard to copy, compounds with data
  • Battle-tested dataset from the only live GPS-denied proving ground on earth
  • OEM design-ins at low SWaP-C & FPV-attach price — a module, not a platform
