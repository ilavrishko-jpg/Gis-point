# GIS-Point × ERPUG 2026 — marketing playbook

Goal: turn the ERPUG 2026 trends into messages, proof and content that bring GIS-Point more qualified leads
(road authorities, municipalities, road-construction & maintenance contractors, engineering consultancies,
reconstruction programmes, IFI-funded projects).

> Assumption: GIS-Point delivers geospatial / road-data services (surveys, mobile mapping, GIS integration,
> asset & condition management). Adjust the proof points below to the exact service catalogue.

---

## 1. Positioning (one sentence)

**"GIS-Point turns one road survey into many decisions — certified-grade data, delivered in days, inside the GIS
your team already uses."**

### Three message pillars (mapped to what the market said at ERPUG)

| Pillar | What buyers worry about | What GIS-Point says | Proof we need to show |
|--------|-------------------------|---------------------|------------------------|
| **1. Survey once, use many times** | Tight budgets; every department buys its own data | One drive → condition, IRI/rutting, assets, signs/markings, 3D/as-built, clearances, (subsurface) | A single project showing ≥5 deliverables from one survey and the cost saved vs. separate contracts |
| **2. Fast & trustworthy** | Data arrives months late; can't verify vendor quality | Turnaround measured in days; repeatability tested; standards-aligned (IRI per ASTM/AASHTO/EN) | Published turnaround KPI; repeat-run statistics; certification or independent benchmark |
| **3. Decisions, not files** | Terabytes nobody can use | GIS/digital-twin platform, dashboards, budget scenarios, AI-assisted with engineer sign-off | Live demo portal; before/after maintenance plan; customer quote |

## 2. What GIS-Point should show the market (content & demo backlog)

1. **"One survey — ten answers" showcase** (hero case study). One road section, all layers on a map: IRI, ruts,
   cracks, potholes, signs, markings, guardrail height, bridge clearance, 360° imagery, as-built deviations.
2. **Turnaround dashboard.** Publicly state "from drive to portal in X days" and track it. Speed was the #1 theme at ERPUG.
3. **Repeatability / accuracy report.** Run the same section 3× and publish the statistics (the PAS 2161 logic).
   Plan a formal certification or third-party benchmark in the next 12 months.
4. **Positioning matters explainer.** Show 10 m vs. cm-level GNSS/INS on the same map — why it matters for
   digital twins, asset registers and contractor payments.
5. **Tiered monitoring offer.** Low-cost frequent monitoring (smartphone/fleet AI) + periodic high-accuracy survey,
   one platform. Pre-empts the "why not just use a phone app?" objection.
6. **Reconstruction & resilience angle** (strong for Ukraine/CEE): rapid damage assessment, prioritisation of
   repair budgets, transparent reporting to donors/IFIs — fast, repeatable, auditable data.
7. **Budget-scenario demo.** "If you have €X, here is the treatment plan and network condition in 5 years."
8. **Beyond motorways:** city streets, bike lanes, gravel/local roads — the long tail of municipal customers.

## 4. Objection handling (from ERPUG debates)

| Objection | Answer |
|-----------|--------|
| "A phone app is cheaper." | Yes — for network screening. For IRI, rut depth, as-built, payments and design you need certified-grade data. We offer both tiers on one platform. |
| "Your data takes months." | Our committed turnaround is X days; here is the dashboard. |
| "How do we know your data is right?" | Repeat-run statistics, standards alignment, independent benchmark/certification roadmap. |
| "We already have a GIS." | We deliver into *your* GIS (open formats, APIs); no lock-in. |
| "Budgets are cut." | One survey replaces several contracts across departments — here's the ROI calculation. |
