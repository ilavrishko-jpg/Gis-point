Why the sudden obsession? Because road owners have been clear: **data that arrives months after the survey is already
out of date.** Potholes have formed, budgets have been allocated, contractors have been chosen.

## Where the time goes

Traditional pipelines lose time in four places:

1. **Planning** — inefficient routes, repeat trips for missed sections.
2. **Capture QA** — errors discovered weeks later in the office.
3. **Transfer** — physically shipping disks with terabytes of data (3D profilers produce ~3 GB per km).
4. **Processing** — software that doesn't use modern hardware.

## How leaders are fixing it

- **Automated route planning** so crews drive the minimum distance.
- **Live QA on a tablet** during capture — no return trips.
- **On-board (edge) processing** and upload over 4G/5G or satellite.
- **GPU-accelerated processing** and better compression.
- **Cloud portals** where the client sees results as soon as they're processed.

## Speed without losing trust

Fast is only useful if it's right. The best teams combine speed with repeatability tests, standards and human QA.
Speed and quality are no longer a trade-off — they are both part of the deliverable.
