## Part 4 — Field Notes and Key Learnings

### How US Venture Capital Actually Works
- Portfolio math: 50% of bets lost, 35% break even, 10% return 2x, only **5% make the fund**. You need to be that 5% — a massive bet, not a safe one.
- Funding stages map to **revenue, not time**: Pre-Seed <$10M raised, $0 revenue · Seed <$4M, ~$500K revenue · Series A <$15M, ~$3M revenue · Series B <$40M, ~$6M revenue. VCs expect a 20-22 month return cycle.
- US investors ask one question: **how fast and how big?** Survival thinking does not land; growth orientation does. Global mindset from day one.
- Fundraising is always happening — a shared co-founder role. Show the division (product / business / relationships) in the deck. Show the US entity; it removes a major blocker.
- VCs fund people they know or who come through someone they trust. **Networking is the mechanism.** Accelerators give three things: narrative, connections, investors.
- Lead with signal-driven evidence (signed pilots, LOIs, recommendations). Show the delta between problem and solution. Spend funds on growth, not longevity.

### How to Build a Pitch That Works
- Two elements: **content** (what) + **delivery** (how). Both matter equally.
- **NABC framework (SRI):** **N**eed first (big market need, exact customer) → **A**pproach (why you win, what's different) → **B**enefits (outcomes, not features) → **C**ompetition (alternatives and why you win). Hook at start, close at end, ask for exactly what you want.
- Three formats: 30-60s intro (hallways/networking) · 5-10 min pitch event (make them want a follow-up) · 30-45 min investor meeting (full picture + Q&A; know who's in the room).
- Know your biggest supporter in the room; pitch to them first. You own your outcome.
- Most important metric: the **delta between problem and solution**. Make it measurable and unavoidable.

### Evidence Is Everything
- Without evidence, nothing else matters. A good experimental result survives the next pivot and market cycle.
- Language shift: not "we're the best on 30% of farms" but "farms with HowCow reduced losses by 80% compared to farms without us." Every investor asks: **compared to what?**
- **Five valid comparison frameworks:** (1) same conditions without vs. with HowCow; (2) before vs. after on the same farm; (3) similar farms without vs. with; (4) different farm sizes (does it scale?); (5) control group vs. intervention.
- **Minimum viable evidence:** 5-6 farms to show a pattern; 20 units for statistical weight; 1-2 lactation cycles or one season (3-4 months minimum). Design the experiment now so results stay meaningful after the next product update.

### AI-Powered Marketing and Visibility
- Marketing is more important than product at this stage. Zero second chances with the same investor.
- Discovery has changed: people research through AI answer engines (ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Google AI) **before** visiting a website. **LinkedIn company pages are among the most cited sources** in AI-generated search.
- Consistency > audience size. Generic AI content is worse than silence.
- **Three-track content system:** Track A = persona portfolio (CEO / investor / farm-owner voices) · Track B = market monitoring (weekly scan of regulations, research, competitor news, Bird Flu signals — original opinions) · Track C = content specialist (style guide + tone-matched prompt per channel). Post 2-3x/week.
- Use **synthetic personas** to stress-test the deck across 7-8 dimensions before any real investor sees it.
- Sharpest current proof point: **HowCow Bird Flu early detection results.** Post it. Put it in the deck.

### AI in Organizations — What Actually Matters
- AI does not fix broken processes — it amplifies them (good and bad). Broken workflows fail faster and more visibly.
- Most dangerous resistance comes from the **middle** of the org — legal, HR, risk, compliance — not leadership or frontline.
- We don't sell AI, we sell **results**. The right question isn't "how much do we cut costs" but "how much additional revenue does the farmer earn because of us."
- Strongest AI-native structure: everyone builds (IC builder/operator), someone owns customer outcomes (DRI), someone owns AI strategy (AI founder). No middle management. Token-maxing over headcount.
- Embed AI in the flow of work — the **Toggle Tax** is real. Keep generating S curves; start the next wave before the previous finishes.

### Carbon Credits, Provenance Milk, and New Revenue Tracks
- The collar is already a **dMRV device**. Behavioral data → methane proxy → carbon credits. Infrastructure to generate **$400-450 per cow per year** exists — needs packaging and a verified methodology.
- **A2 Milk** is the cleanest premium opportunity: sells at 2x price in US/Australia/China. Problem: no reliable verification of A2/A2 cows. HowCow knows every cow → tie genotype to collar ID → blockchain-verified A2 certificates per batch.
- **Terroir Milk** (longer-term provenance play): wine provenance applied to dairy — GPS, breed, diet, season tied to a verified fat-protein fingerprint.
- **Blockchain Milk:** QR code with full cow + farm history per batch for buyer transparency.

### Data Strategy and ML Foundations
- **FAIR data:** Findable, Accessible, Interoperable, Reusable. Raw data without context is noise.
- **JSON → Markdown:** human-first, agent-friendly structured text.
- **CRISP-DM** loop: Business Understanding → Data Understanding → Data Preparation → Modeling → Evaluation → Deployment → back to Data Understanding.
- **Dataiku build priority for HowCow:** (1) raw collar data as Dataset 1 (temperature, activity, rumination); (2) Prepare Recipe to calculate deviation from each cow's baseline; (3) anomaly score per cow per day as Dataset 2; (4) classification model (score above threshold → alert); (5) daily batch scoring recipe; (6) output dataset with risk scores for the CRM app. Target **AUC > 0.85**. Dataiku trial closed June 2, 2026.
- Always ask: **compared to what?** Establish the baseline first.

### Negotiation — Core Principles
- Negotiation is **communication** — not combat, not performance.
- Two core tensions: creating vs. distributing value (cooperation + competition at once); empathy vs. assertiveness.
- Never mistake a stated **position** for the actual **interest**. The deal gets done at the level of interests, not positions.
- You can cooperate and compete simultaneously — that's the craft.
