# Session Summary: Gigi Wang
## Pitching Anything to Anybody: Audience, Story, Delivery, and the NABC Framework

> Source: `2026-05-13-Gigi Wang.pdf` — Berkeley AgriTech Accelerator

---

## Core Message
Pitching is not presenting information — it is **communicating in a way that gets someone to take action** (a follow-up, a pilot, an investment, an intro). The goal is not to "explain everything" but to make the audience care enough to want the next step. Busy investors/customers reward clarity, relevance, confidence, and emotional connection — not completeness.

## Key Theses
- **Know your audience.** Customer wants benefit to them; investor wants returns; partner wants why collaboration makes sense; farmer wants less pain / better outcomes / lower risk / protected margins. Start with *why the listener should care*, not what you want to say.
- **Pitch = Content + Delivery.** Same content succeeds or fails on tone, structure, energy. **Three P's of delivery: Pitch (vary voice), Pause (let ideas land), Pace (vary speed).** Monotone = a lullaby.
- **Use story.** "I worked in IT, then returned to my village and applied tech to our family farm" beats "I've farmed 5 years." Story = identity, motivation, credibility.
- **NABC framework (SRI):** **N**eed (why care?) → **A**pproach (your solution/tech/model) → **B**enefits (measurable value) → **C**ompetition (alternatives + why you win). Need comes first; A/B/C order can flex (showing Competition first can make your solution look better — e.g. $100K system vs. a $1B airport terminal).
- **Hook and Close.** Don't launch before the audience is ready — open with a hook. Always close with a clear ask. A perfect pitch without a close is wasted.
- **Tools:** analogies ("Zocdoc for pets"), "imagine…" to visualize scale, show your solution is *dramatically* better (Uber vs. taxi), FUD where stakes are real, "connect the dots" (don't assume the audience knows why it matters).
- **Three pitch types:** 30-sec intro (hallway/networking) · pitch-event pitch (earn a follow-up) · long pitch (45-min meeting → present <half, leave room to listen; conversation is where value emerges).
- **Take feedback well** — never "no, no, that's wrong" (looks uncoachable); listen, ask why, extract the bias/signal.
- Investor quote: *"Make me fall in love with you. If you make me fall in love with you, I'll throw money at you."*
- **SRI Five Disciplines of Innovation:** Need · Value creation · Champion · Team · Organizational alignment.

*Closing line: "No farmers, no food."*
