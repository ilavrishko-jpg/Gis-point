# GIS-Point industries & verticals

What GIS-Point can do, grouped by business vertical. Each product id maps to an entry in
`catalog.yaml`. A product can appear under more than one vertical.

## Geospatial & Mapping
Interactive 2D/3D web and mobile maps, 360° street-view and virtual tours, LiDAR point-cloud and
CAD data processing, georeferencing, and self-hosted open-source mapping stacks for any organization
that works with location data.
`ancestral-trees` · `eagle-eye` · `makani` · `mangrove` · `orange-gis` · `point-cloud` · `suitability` · `geo-oss` · `viewers-3d` · `aerial-georef` · `allgis` · `h3-hexagons` · `riyadh-pano-ai` · `splat-3dtiles` · `geo-ai` · `custom-map` · `geoserver-mini` · `3d-model-viewer` · `geo-data-utils`

## Environmental & Remote Sensing
Turns satellite imagery, soil sampling and drone data into vegetation and land-condition insights,
management zones, and site-evaluation reports for farms, land managers and agencies.
`site-monitoring` · `soil-hub` · `agrizone` · `site-infra-eval` · `soil-raster-processing`

## Agriculture / AgTech
Precision-farming platforms that map soil types, track crop and field health from satellite data, and
delineate variable-rate treatment zones to cut input costs and raise yield.
`soil-hub` · `site-monitoring` · `agrizone` · `soil-raster-processing`

## Earth Observation & Satellite Imagery
Search, preview, order and process satellite and street-level imagery from multiple providers, with
area-of-interest drawing and large-scale AI processing pipelines.
`skypulse` · `site-monitoring` · `riyadh-pano-ai`

## Weather & Meteorology
Interactive weather maps with location-based wind, temperature, rainfall and gust predictions for
agriculture, marine, energy and outdoor sectors.
`weather`

## Infrastructure & Asset Monitoring
Remotely survey and monitor roads, utilities, structures and construction sites — tracking ground
movement, deformation and as-built deviations on interactive maps.
`subsidence` · `eagle-eye` · `site-infra-eval` · `ai-hemaya` · `road-damage-detection` · `slope-detection`

## Location Services / GovTech
Digital addressing platforms that assign short, shareable location codes to any point on the map so
hard-to-find places can be located by delivery, emergency and everyday users.
`makani`

## Real Estate & Land / Site Selection
Multi-criteria suitability analysis that scores and ranks locations to help planners, developers and
investors decide where to build, invest or place facilities.
`suitability` · `soil-hub` · `agrizone`

## AI / Computer Vision & Automation
AI systems for object detection, LiDAR classification, image matching, browser-based inference, and
autonomous browser agents that extract and act on data.
`lidar-ai-cluster` · `object-detection` · `rt-object-detection-web` · `aerial-georef` · `athlo` · `riyadh-pano-ai` · `site-infra-eval` · `geo-ai` · `road-damage-detection` · `slope-detection`

## 3D / Reality Capture & Digital Twins
Photorealistic 3D captures (Gaussian splatting), point-cloud/mesh viewing, and 3D-tile streaming in
the browser and on a virtual globe.
`allgis` · `splat-3dtiles` · `viewers-3d` · `point-cloud` · `ai-hemaya` · `3d-model-viewer`

## Defence & Autonomous Systems
GPS-denied UAV navigation research and marketing/lead-capture sites for defence and autonomous-drone
products that must operate where GPS is jammed or spoofed.
`ons-landing` · `uav-drone`

## Physical Security & Surveillance
3D site-monitoring platforms that track people and assets indoors via wearable tags and unify live
CCTV feeds and camera-coverage analysis on a single interactive model.
`ai-hemaya`

## Fitness & Wellness
Gym and studio management SaaS with class scheduling, memberships, Stripe payments and payouts, and
AI agents that sync bookings across ~198 third-party fitness platforms.
`athlo`

## Healthcare / Digital Care
Connected patient-care platforms covering appointments, medications, health goals, questionnaires,
billing and reminders for clinics and care organizations.
`instacare`

## Education / EdTech
LMS platforms for schools that manage courses, students, teachers and automated assessment with
grading, attendance and MIS integration across multiple schools.
`curriculum`

## Fintech / Payments
Regulated digital-wallet and payment platforms with double-entry ledgers, KYC/AML compliance and
multi-bank integration, plus cross-border payroll cost calculation engines.
`digital-wallet` · `acumen`

## Logistics & Freight
Multi-sided freight marketplaces (load boards) connecting shippers, carriers and drivers with
bidding, live GPS tracking, proof-of-delivery and government e-waybill compliance. Plus
self-hosted routing and turn-by-turn directions with editable road data.
`eload` · `custom-route`

## Legal / Document Automation
Self-hosted electronic-signature systems for signing, tracking and storing contracts and forms
digitally, avoiding per-signature SaaS fees while retaining full data control.
`esignature`

## Corporate Web & Marketing
Modern multi-language, CMS-driven marketing sites with SEO tooling, lead-capture forms and analytics —
for GIS-Point's own presence and for client product launches.
`web-presence` · `ons-landing`

## Automation & Internal Tools
Chatbots and internal tools that automate data collection, reporting, dashboards and alerts —
plus reusable geospatial/data-conversion utilities for ETL between formats and coordinate systems.
`telegram-accountant` · `geo-data-utils` · `soil-raster-processing`
