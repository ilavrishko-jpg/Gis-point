## 1. What AI / automation features has each vendor shipped or announced?

### Takeaway
Between 2024 and Nov 2026 every major vendor moved from "AI features" to an **agent/assistant layer on top of a cloud data platform** (Autodesk Assistant + neural CAD, Bentley Copilot + Infrastructure AI, Trimble Assist + Agent Studio, Nemetschek AI Assistant, Procore/Datagrid agents, DroneDeploy Progress/Safety/Inspection AI, Revizto MCP server). For Scan-to-BIM specifically, what actually shipped is narrower: **AI point-cloud classification, scan-to-mesh, 360-video-to-point-cloud, and scan-vs-BIM deviation/progress tracking** — fully automatic point-cloud-to-parametric-BIM was not announced as a shipped product by any of the major vendors in the material found.

### Inferences
- "Scan-to-BIM automation" in vendor roadmaps is being delivered as **components** (classification → mesh/segmentation → guided extraction → deviation check) rather than one-click BIM; the manual modelling step survives, which is why Hexagon (Voyansi), Matterport and others still sell **human-in-the-loop BIM services** alongside software.
- **360-video-to-point-cloud** (Trimble 360 Reality Capture, Cintoo 360 Edition, Cupix, OpenSpace, ProjectSight 360) and **Gaussian splats** (Bentley/Cesium, Pix4D, Esri) are the two capture-side technology shifts of 2025–2026; both lower capture cost and widen the funnel of "good enough" reality data.
- MCP has become the de facto agent-interop hook in AEC (Bentley, Revizto, SketchUp connector), which reduces lock-in at the agent layer even where data platforms remain proprietary.

## 2. Where is each steering customers — cloud platforms, lock-in vs. open standards, subscription/consumption pricing?

### Takeaway
All four big design vendors are steering users to **vendor "industry clouds"** (Autodesk Forma, Bentley Infrastructure Cloud/iTwin+Cesium, Trimble Connect, Hexagon GeoCloud/Octave) with subscription-only licensing and emerging **consumption/outcome pricing for AI and APIs**. Openness is real but selective: Bentley (3D Tiles via Cesium, MCP) and Revizto market "data freedom"; Autodesk states Forma will eventually replace Revit, which concentrates data in its cloud.

### Inferences
- The decisive lock-in vector is shifting from file formats (RVT) to **cloud data models + AI agents trained/operating on them**; Autodesk's "Forma replaces Revit" trajectory means as-built/scan-derived models will increasingly be expected to land in Forma/ACC rather than as RVT files.
- 3D Tiles (Bentley/Cesium, Google, Esri-compatible) is emerging as the neutral **streaming** format for reality data, while IFC remains the neutral **exchange** format; IFC 5 is still foundational/in development as of mid-2025–2026, so IFC 4.3 remains the practical deliverable standard through the near term.
- Consumption pricing (Flex tokens, API credits, AI add-ons like SketchUp's $11.99/mo) makes service providers' software cost more variable and tied to automation volume.
