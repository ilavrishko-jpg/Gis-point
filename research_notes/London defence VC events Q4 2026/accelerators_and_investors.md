So every finding below comes from search-engine result snippets, not from reading the full pages. The labels mean:
- **VERIFIED**: the snippet came from an official or organiser page (or the organiser's own news post), and it is clearly about the 2026 edition.
- **UNCONFIRMED**: the information comes only from an aggregator or third-party site, the page is not specific to 2026, or the date is my own inference.

## Q1: Accelerators, incubators and programmes with London events or deadlines, Oct–Dec 2026

### Takeaway
Most of the London activity falls in the week of 1–8 October 2026: London Defence Tech Week, which includes the EDTH Defense Tech Days and hackathon, the Resilience Conference (5–6 Oct) and the Deep Tech London Summit (8 Oct). The big 2026 application windows have mostly **closed already**:
- NATO DIANA 2027 call: closed 3 July 2026
- UKDI Security Open Call Cycle 1: closed 27 Aug 2026
- UKDI ISO round 8: closes 22 Sept 2026
- Techstars London Fall 2026: closed 14 Sept 2026

For Q4 the realistic routes are: the next UKDI open-call cycles (dates not yet published), the London Defence Tech Week events, and preparing for 2027 application rounds.

**NATO DIANA (headquartered in London)**
- The 2027 Challenge Call opened 2 June 2026 and **closed 3 July 2026**. The accelerator starts in **January 2027**. Selected companies receive €100k, with up to €300k more through the Mission Track (up to €400k in total). The call requires TRL 4 or above; universities and non-profits cannot apply. The six challenge areas are: human survivability; multidomain autonomy of uncrewed systems; sensing and data processing for ISR; operational resilience in contested environments; responsive logistics; and scalable air-defence countermeasures. **VERIFIED** (official challenges page plus multiple secondary sources): [diana.nato.int/challenges](https://www.diana.nato.int/challenges.html); [DSEI Gateway](https://dsei-gateway.com/en/news/sme/nato-diana-launches-2027-challenge-calls/); [Inventya](https://www.inventya.com/calls/nato-diana-2027-challenge-call)
- The NATO DIANA Capital Network launched in April 2026. It is a NATO-screened network of investors (sovereign funds, VCs and family offices) that back DIANA companies. **VERIFIED** (reported by EU-Startups, Sept 2026): [EU-Startups](https://www.eu-startups.com/2026/09/the-21-european-investors-funding-natos-defence/)
- DIANA's headquarters are in London: [NATO topic page](https://www.nato.int/en/about-us/organization/nato-structure/defence-innovation-accelerator-for-the-north-atlantic-diana)

## Q2: Pre-seed/seed investors backing defence tech in the UK, and their London events in Q4 2026

### Takeaway
The defence-focused firms with confirmed UK presence and recent activity are:
- **NSSIF**, the UK government's investment fund for national security and defence
- **NATO Innovation Fund**, which has a UK office
- **Expeditions**, based in London and Warsaw, which closed a €197M Fund II in July 2026
- **Keen Venture Partners**, based in Amsterdam and London, with a €180M defence fund

Generalist firms with confirmed 2026 defence deals are Lakestar, Seedcamp, Plural, Lux, Northzone and Amadeus. The only confirmed public London appearance in Q4 2026 is at London Defence Tech Week (NIF and Amadeus). I found no fund-hosted office hours for Oct–Dec.

### Cited Findings

**NSSIF (National Security Strategic Investment Fund)**, part of the British Business Bank
- NSSIF is the UK government's investment fund for dual-use technology in national security and defence. It invests commercially alongside other investors. Its focus areas are AI, data, autonomy and robotics, quantum, semiconductors, space, telecoms and engineering biology. It has been allocated **up to £330m for FY2026-27 to 2029-30**. [British Business Bank](https://www.british-business-bank.co.uk/finance-options/equity-finance/national-security-strategic-investment-fund); [nssif.gov.uk](https://www.nssif.gov.uk/about-us); [Parliament written question](https://questions-statements.parliament.uk/written-questions/detail/2025-11-24/93942)
- Stage: it co-invests and is typically seed-plus/Series A, not pre-seed. This is an inference from its co-investment model.
- Events: NSSIF was listed as an exhibitor at Security & Policing 2026 (held in March, not London Q4): [S&P listing](https://www.securityandpolicing.co.uk/exhibitors/exhibitors-list-2026/national-security-strategic-investment-fund-nssif/). I found **no Q4 2026 London event**.

**NATO Innovation Fund (NIF)**
- A €1bn fund backed by 24 Allies. It invests from Seed to Series B, with initial cheques up to €15m, and also runs a fund-of-funds programme. Its headquarters are in the Netherlands, it is domiciled in Luxembourg, and it has **regional offices in the UK** and Poland. [nif.fund](https://www.nif.fund/); [about](https://www.nif.fund/about/); [defencecal summary](https://defencecal.com/funds/nato-innovation-fund)
- 2026 activity: participated in Kraken (London), Greenjets and Uplift360; led Kelluu's €15M Series A: [EU-Startups](https://www.eu-startups.com/2026/09/the-21-european-investors-funding-natos-defence/). Co-led Terasi's €11M round (Sept 2026): [Tech.eu](https://tech.eu/2026/09/22/nato-innovation-fund-co-leads-eur11m-investment-in-defencetech-outfit-terasi). Invested as an LP in Expeditions Fund II: [NIF news](https://www.nif.fund/news/nato-innovation-fund-backs-e197m-expeditions-fund-ii-to-champion-defence-tech-founders-early-support-scaling/)
- Events: Erin Hallock of NIF is named as giving input to London Defence Tech Week (1–8 Oct 2026). **VERIFIED** (organiser post): [Resilience Media](https://resiliencemedia.co/the-london-defence-tech-week-programme-is-here/)
- NIF also publishes the report "Defence, Security and Resilience in Europe 2026": [PDF](https://www.nif.fund/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/NIF-report-Defence-Security-and-Resilience-2026-A4-size-25mm-margin-V2.pdf)
