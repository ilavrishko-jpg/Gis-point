Method note: research date 25 Sep 2026. Direct page fetches were blocked by the network egress proxy (summit.sifted.eu, ff.co and wise.com all returned EGRESS_BLOCKED), so every fact below comes from search-engine snippets of the cited pages. VERIFIED means a 2026-specific date appeared in a snippet from the official or organiser page. It does not mean I read the full page. Before relying on prices, venues or speaker names, re-check them on the live page.

## Q1: Big conferences: which fall in London in Oct to Dec 2026, and which should be excluded?

### Takeaway
Few of the big "general" conferences on the list actually land in London in Q4 2026. The London items that do are Sifted Summit (only its last day, 1 Oct), SaaStr AI London (~1 Dec), UKBAA's early-stage summit (23 Nov) and Investment Futures deep-tech showcase (24 Nov), LTW365 AI in Critical Infrastructure (24 Nov), and the LVCN Winter Ball (3 Dec). Most of the famous names (Founders Forum Global, London Tech Week, SuperVenture, BVCA Summit, Tech Show London, Investor Summit, Accelerate) fall outside the window. The Global Investment Summit has reportedly been pushed to spring 2027 in Manchester, and Pitch@Palace no longer exists.

### Inferences
- The strongest Q4 London window for a defence/dual-use pre-seed founder is 1 to 11 Oct: Deep Tech Week, EDTH Defense Tech Days, the Resilience Conference and the Deep Tech London Summit, with Sifted Summit ending on 1 Oct. It is only 1 to 2 weeks from the research date, so action is urgent.

### Inferences
