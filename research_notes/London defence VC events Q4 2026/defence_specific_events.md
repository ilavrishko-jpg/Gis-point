# Defence-tech, dual-use and national-security founder–investor events in London, Oct–Dec 2026

Method note: research done 25 Sep 2026. The egress proxy blocked direct fetches of the main official sites (eurodefense.tech, londondefenceconference.com, defence-disrupted.co.uk, londondefencetechweek.com). The findings below come from search-engine snippets of those official pages, plus organiser news posts (Resilience Media), Luma and X. VERIFIED means a 2026-dated official or organiser page or post confirms the date. It does not mean the whole page was read. Before relying on venue and price details, check them on the official page.

## Which defence/dual-use/natsec events in London Oct–Dec 2026 exist?

### Takeaway
Early October has the most in one place. **London Defence Tech Week runs 1–8 Oct 2026.** Inside it sit the Resilience Conference (5–6 Oct), EDTH Defense Tech Days with a hackathon and a VC and founders night, and Deep Tech London (5–11 Oct, with a summit on 8 Oct). After that week, the only confirmed defence-investor event in London is the **LDC Investment Forum on 2 Dec 2026** in the City. I found no confirmed defence-specific founder–investor events in London for Nov–Dec apart from that one.

### Cited Findings

**1. London Defence Tech Week: 1–8 Oct 2026. VERIFIED**
- The week runs 1–8 Oct and includes conferences, networking events, workshops, investor gatherings and partner events across London. The organisers say it is designed to connect defence needs with "founders, investors, industry leaders and military decision-makers". — [Resilience Media: programme is here](https://resiliencemedia.co/the-london-defence-tech-week-programme-is-here/); [Official site](https://www.londondefencetechweek.com/)
- "Some events are open to register, others require an application, and some are invitation only." Independent events include hackathons, investor and founder gatherings, dinners, panels, demonstrations and breakfast sessions. — [Resilience Media](https://resiliencemedia.co/the-london-defence-tech-week-programme-is-here/)
- Organiser: Resilience Media, the team behind the Resilience Conference. — [Announcing London Defence Tech Week](https://www.resiliencemedia.co/p/announcing-london-defence-tech-week)

**2. Resilience Conference London 2026: 5–6 Oct 2026, plus a private Day Three on 7 Oct. VERIFIED (dates)**
- Takes place 5–6 Oct 2026 in London. It brings investors together with military leaders, policymakers, founders and industry executives. The aim is to connect NATO military and national security communities with the tech sector. — [Resilience Media: Leading Defence Investors Join](https://resiliencemedia.co/179449784-2/); [resilienceconference.io](https://www.resilienceconference.io/)
- Day Three on 7 Oct is a private programme on cognitive warfare and societal resilience. — [Resilience Media](https://resiliencemedia.co/the-london-defence-tech-week-programme-is-here/)
- Pitch or showcase formats: LAUNCH@RC on 5 Oct and SCALE@RC on 6 Oct. Together they are described as "a unique opportunity to meet 20 of the most exciting companies in the sector". — [Resilience Media: Something new, something BIG](https://resiliencemedia.co/something-new-something-big-at-resilience-conference-london/)
- Described as "small, high-level, and secure". Tickets via resilienceconference.io, also listed by techUK. — [techUK listing](https://www.techuk.org/what-we-deliver/events/resilience-conference.html)
- Agenda published. — [Resilience Media: Agenda is live](https://resiliencemedia.co/the-resilience-conference-london-2026-agenda-is-live/)
- Venue and ticket price: not found in the snippets. This is a gap.

**3. EDTH (European Defense Tech Hub) Defense Tech Days, London: 1–8 Oct 2026. VERIFIED (dates); sub-event details partly unverified**
- Brings together "engineers, founders, investors, government, and military from the UK, Ukraine, and across Europe". Leads up to the Resilience Conference (5–6 Oct) and Deep Tech London (5–11 Oct). — [EDTH London 2026 page](https://eurodefense.tech/london-2026/)
- The programme includes a **VC & Founders Networking Night**, the **European Defense Tech Hackathon** and **The New Defense Dinner**. — [Resilience Media](https://resiliencemedia.co/the-london-defence-tech-week-programme-is-here/)
- A search snippet puts the EDTH hackathon at 2–4 Oct. Deep Tech London also mentions a Counter-UAS forum and a "Build with Ukraine" strand. — [EDTH London 2026](https://eurodefense.tech/london-2026/); [deeptech.london](https://www.deeptech.london/)
- Not to be confused with an earlier EDTH London hackathon on 26–28 Jun 2026, which is outside the window. — [Luma: EDTH London (summer)](https://luma.com/edth-2026-summer-london)
- Exact date and venue of the VC & Founders Night: not found. Tickets are usually handled through Luma or the EDTH community.

**4. Deep Tech London / Deep Tech Week London: 5–11 Oct 2026. Summit Thursday 8 Oct 2026. VERIFIED. Dual-use, not defence-only**
- Summit on 8 Oct 2026, 11:00–18:30, Brick Lane, Shoreditch. Tickets £100. Aimed at founders, scientists, investors and corporates. Run in partnership with EDTH and Resilience Media. — [deeptech.london](https://www.deeptech.london/); [Luma](https://luma.com/deeptechldn26); [EventsList](https://eventslist.co.uk/london/events/deep-tech-london-summit-2026-10-08)
- Side event: "Deep Tech London: The KO with Singular + Seedcamp" on Tue 6 Oct. — [Resilience Media](https://resiliencemedia.co/the-london-defence-tech-week-programme-is-here/); [Deep Tech Week London 2026](https://www.deep-tech-week.com/london-2026)

**5. Sifted Summit 2026: 30 Sep – 1 Oct 2026, London. VERIFIED. General tech with a defence track; only 1 Oct falls in the window**
- The agenda covers defence-tech supply-chain risk, "what the landscape looks like for investors", and building a business on defence revenue. 131 speakers. — [Sifted Summit](https://summit.sifted.eu/?page=4); [2026 Agenda](https://summit.sifted.eu/2026-agenda)

**6. LDC Investment Forum 2026 (London Defence Conference): 2 Dec 2026, City of London. VERIFIED (date, via the organiser's official X account)**
- "The LDC Investment Forum, the annual winter gathering of the @LondonDefConf, will take place on 2 December in the heart of the City." Theme: "Funding Our Future Defence". Brings together policymakers, military, investors and industry. — [London Defence Conference on X](https://x.com/LondonDefConf/status/2095460146528805295); [londondefenceconference.com](https://londondefenceconference.com/)
- Enquiries go to info@londondefenceconference.com. It appears to be invite-based or require registration. Venue and price not found.
- This is a policy and finance forum. It is not a pitch event. Founders should expect a large-fund and institutional audience rather than angels.

## Which include pitch sessions, investor office hours or curated founder–investor matchmaking?

### Takeaway
Confirmed formats: Resilience Conference LAUNCH@RC and SCALE@RC (company showcases), the EDTH VC & Founders Networking Night and hackathon demo, and application-gated events across London Defence Tech Week. I found no confirmed investor office hours.

### Cited Findings
- LAUNCH@RC (5 Oct) and SCALE@RC (6 Oct) showcase 20 companies. LAUNCH suggests an early-stage track, so it is likely the most relevant format for pre-seed. — [Resilience Media](https://resiliencemedia.co/something-new-something-big-at-resilience-conference-london/)
- EDTH VC & Founders Networking Night, hackathon (with demos) and New Defense Dinner. — [Resilience Media](https://resiliencemedia.co/the-london-defence-tech-week-programme-is-here/)
- London Defence Tech Week events are a mix: open registration, application-only and invitation-only. — [Resilience Media](https://resiliencemedia.co/the-london-defence-tech-week-programme-is-here/)
- The Seedcamp + Singular "KO" session on 6 Oct is an investor-hosted event. Its format (pitch or networking) is not stated. — [Resilience Media](https://resiliencemedia.co/the-london-defence-tech-week-programme-is-here/)

### Inferences
- How LAUNCH@RC companies are selected (application window, whether it is still open) is not known. Founders should contact Resilience Media at once, because the event is about 10 days away.

### Gaps
- Application deadlines, and whether LAUNCH@RC or the EDTH hackathon still accept entries, could not be checked because the official pages were blocked.
