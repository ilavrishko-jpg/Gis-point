Research date: 28 Sep 2026. Method: web search only. Direct page fetches for charlestondca.org, events.sofwerx.org, deftech.nc.gov, defensetechweek.com and luma.com were **blocked by the network egress proxy**, so event details come from search-result snippets of those official pages. No page could be opened in full. Labels: VERIFIED = date, venue and organiser all confirmed by a primary/official source snippet. PARTLY VERIFIED = date confirmed but venue, organiser or investor presence not confirmed, or the source is an aggregator. UNCONFIRMED = not confirmed for 2026 or the date is unknown.

## A. Southeast defence, dual-use and VC events, 9–20 Nov 2026

### Takeaway
The Southeast has very few investor-facing defence events in the 9–20 Nov 2026 window. The flagship events all fall outside it: eMerge Americas, Miami Tech Week and Israel Tech Week's DefenseTech Summit were in April 2026, and CDCA's Eastern Defense Summit is 8–10 Dec 2026. SOFWERX's small-business and investor speed-dating event is on 5 Nov. The in-window events are mostly government or technical, such as the Huntsville hypersonics summit and a SOFWERX USV assessment, or generic tech events such as NC TECH Awards and the Space Coast Expo. None of the in-window events has a confirmed investor list.

## B. East Coast investors backing pre-seed and seed defence, dual-use and national-security startups

### Takeaway
Sourced details (HQ, stage, check size) were found for only part of the list: Shield Capital, Scout Ventures, Red Cell Partners, America's Frontier Fund, Paladin Capital, Snowpoint Ventures, J2 Ventures, D3, Network.VC Defense and Freedom Fund. Most US defence funds invest through a US entity; that is inferred, not stated by any source I could open. The most direct routes for Ukrainian founders are the Ukraine-focused vehicles (D3, Network.VC Defense, Freedom Fund, UAID Fund) and the Brave1 US Roadshow investor network. **No investor is confirmed to attend any 9–20 Nov 2026 Southeast event.**

### Cited Findings
- **Shield Capital**: stages pre-seed, seed, seed+ and Series A. Its stated range is $100K–$5M, with a sweet spot of about $1.5M (third-party profile). — [Superscout](https://superscout.co/investor/shield-capital); [Shield Capital](https://shieldcap.com/)
- **Harpoon Ventures** (Larsen Jensen) has co-invested with Shield Capital and IQT on national-security applications. It runs **Black Flag**, a programme and investor list for critical-tech startups. It is San Diego / West Coast based, so it is not East Coast. — [Business of San Diego](https://businessofsandiego.substack.com/p/inside-harpoons-black-flag-the-top); [VC Sheet](https://www.vcsheet.com/fund/harpoon-ventures)
- **Scout Ventures** (NYC): early dual-use. Typical initial checks are reported at **$2M–$5M**, which is larger than true pre-seed. — [New Market Pitch](https://newmarketpitch.com/blogs/news/defense-tech-top-investors)
- Shield, Scout, Decisive Point, Razor's Edge and Harpoon are named as specialist early-stage defence investors. — [New Market Pitch](https://newmarketpitch.com/blogs/news/defense-tech-top-investors)
- **Red Cell Partners**, Washington DC area, founded 2020. It incubates and funds national-security companies. Checks are about **$1M–$5M** and assets under management about $200M. It invested in Claros (Seed II) on 19 Mar 2026. — [Investor Match](https://investormatch.pro/vcs/red-cell-partners); [CB Insights](https://www.cbinsights.com/investor/red-cell-partners); [Red Cell companies](https://www.redcellpartners.com/companies/)
- **J2 Ventures** (Boston): led by military veterans. It invests in dual-use areas (health monitoring, advanced computing, secure infrastructure), not weapons. — [VC Sheet](https://www.vcsheet.com/fund/j2-ventures)
- **Razor's Edge Ventures** (Reston VA / DC area): has a portfolio page. No 2026 deal details were found. — [Razor's Edge investments](https://razorsvc.com/investments.html)
- **America's Frontier Fund**, Arlington VA, founded 2021. Sectors: semiconductors, advanced manufacturing, AI, space and energy. Latest deal: Venus Aerospace, 8 Jul 2026. — [PitchBook](https://pitchbook.com/profiles/investor/509242-24); [AFF funds](https://americasfrontier.com/funds)
- **Paladin Capital Group** (Washington DC), founded 2001 by former NSA, CIA and DARPA leaders. It focuses on cyber, AI safety and critical infrastructure. In 2026 it led an investment in RADICL (Jan) and led a **$4M seed** in Dam Secure. — [F4 Fund](https://f4.fund/firms/paladin-capital-group)
- **Snowpoint Ventures**, **Palm Beach FL**, founded 2021. It invests in dual-use data, deep-tech and frontier-tech companies. It is the only Southeast-based fund in the list. — [Snowpoint](https://www.snowpoint.com/)
- **D3 (Dare to Defend Democracy)**: early-stage defence-tech fund founded in 2023 by Eveline Buchatskiy, Anton Verkhovodov and Elya Chiechienieva. It is Kyiv-based and invests in startups from Ukraine and allied countries. Its typical check is **~$125K**. The fund grew from $10M to over $30M, and Denmark's EIFO invested $5M. It explicitly backs Ukrainian founders. — [D3](https://d3.vc/about/); [EIFO](https://eifo.dk/en/knowledge-hub/eifo-invests-in-the-venture-fund-d3-to-boost-ukrainian-and-european-innovation-in-defence); [sUAS News](https://www.suasnews.com/2025/07/eifo-invests-5-million-in-d3-the-ukraine-focused-defence-technology-venture-fund/); [The Defender](https://thedefender.media/en/2025/05/d3-projects-out-of-shadows/)
- **Network.VC Defense** (US-Ukrainian): its checks start at **$25K**. It invested in the pre-seed round of DoD Solution (Estonian-Ukrainian; round of $1.1M, June 2026). — [AIN](https://en.ain.ua/2026/06/04/defense-tech-startup-dod-solution-raised-investment-from-networkvc-defense-the-funds-typical-checks-start-at-25000/); [Dealroom](https://app.dealroom.co/news/feed/estonian-ukrainian-defence-tech-startup-dod-solution-secures-funding-from-network-vc-defense-in-1-1m-pre-seed-round)

- **UAID Fund** (US-based): invested in the pre-seed round of Ukrainian startup G-Next Intelligence (Apr 2026). — [InVenture](https://inventure.com.ua/en/news/ukraine/us-based-uaid-fund-invests-in-ukrainian-defense-tech-startup-g-next-intelligence)

### Inferences
- Attendance at 9–20 Nov 2026 events: **no investor on the list is confirmed** for any Southeast event in the window. DC-area investors (Red Cell, Paladin, AFF, Razor's Edge, IQT, Shield) are the most likely to be at DC events that week (Defense Tech Week 16–20 Nov if held in DC, and Baird on 17 Nov). This is speculation, not a confirmed attendance.
- For a Ukrainian founder, the realistic pre-seed checks come from Network.VC Defense ($25K+), D3 (~$125K), Freedom Fund and UAID. The US specialist funds (Shield, Scout, Red Cell) write $1M+ checks and usually need US-customer traction. A Delaware C-corp is typically expected, but no source confirmed it for a specific fund.
