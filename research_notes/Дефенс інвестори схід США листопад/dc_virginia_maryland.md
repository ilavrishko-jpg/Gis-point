The network egress proxy blocked direct loading of almost every official event site (defensetechweek.com, defensescoop.com, bairdconferences.com, events.techconnect.org, luma.com/lu.ma, deep-tech-week.com, youngstartup.com). Many of those snippets are indexed text from the official pages, but I could not open the pages to check ticket prices, speaker lists or registration forms. Because of this, nothing is marked fully VERIFIED unless a search-indexed snippet of the official page gave both the date and the venue. The report writer should treat cost and investor-attendance fields as gaps unless a source is cited.

Status key: VERIFIED = date and venue come from an indexed snippet of the official site. PARTLY VERIFIED = the date comes from the official site but the venue or other details are missing. UNCONFIRMED = no 2026 date found.

## Q1: Which defence and natsec conferences run in DC/NoVA/MD between 9 and 20 Nov 2026?

### Takeaway
Only two defence-industry events with an investor audience are confirmed inside the window. **Defense Tech Week (DefenseScoop), 16–20 Nov 2026** is in Washington, but the venue is not confirmed. **Baird's 9th Annual Defense, Space & Government Conference** is on **17 Nov 2026** at the Ritz-Carlton, Washington DC, and is by invitation only. Most other major events checked fall outside the window: the Reagan Forum, Defense One Tech Summit, NDIA ETI, Defense TechConnect, SCSP AI+ Expo, Hill & Valley, Deep Tech Week DC, DC Startup & Tech Week and the Accelerate Investor Conference. For AUSA follow-ons, NSIN and AFCEA DC-area events, no November 2026 date was found.

## Q4: Which DC-area investors are active at pre-seed and seed in defence?

### Takeaway
Sources found only generic lists. There is a DC city-backed pre-seed/seed fund (the DC Venture Capital Program, administered by K Street Capital). Aggregators name Outlander VC, Quest Venture Partners and 2048 Ventures among DC enterprise pre-seed investors. Defence-specific seed investors in DC could not be confirmed individually.

### Cited Findings
- The DC Venture Capital Program is a $26M District fund investing in pre-seed, seed and early-stage DC-based tech companies. Private investors match it at least 1:1, for a minimum of $52M in total. K Street Capital is the programme administrator — [DC DMPED](https://dmped.dc.gov/page/dc-venture-capital-program); [WDCEP](https://wdcep.com/resources/dc-venture-capital-fund/)
- NFX Signal lists Outlander VC, Quest Venture Partners and 2048 Ventures among top enterprise pre-seed investors in DC. They are not defence-specific — [NFX Signal](https://signal.nfx.com/investor-lists/top-enterprise-pre-seed-washington-d-c-investors)
- The DC/MD/NoVA ecosystem is described as strong in national security, cyber and govtech — [Finta](https://finta.ai/blog/washington-dc-vc-firms-angel-investors)
- National defence-tech VCs frequently cited include Shield Capital, a16z, Founders Fund, Lux, DCVC and 8VC. New defence funds in 2025 included Booz Allen Ventures (McLean VA), Point72 and JPMorgan's Security and Resiliency Initiative — [OpenVC](https://www.openvc.app/investor-lists/defensetech-investors); [Defense News](https://www.defensenews.com/industry/2026/01/20/defense-tech-startups-had-their-best-funding-year-ever-in-2025/)
- Washingtonian profiled "DC's 2026 Leading Defense-Tech Innovators" (4 Aug 2026). This could be a lead source for local founders and investors, but it was not opened — [Washingtonian](https://washingtonian.com/2026/08/04/meet-dcs-2026-leading-defense-tech-innovators/)
