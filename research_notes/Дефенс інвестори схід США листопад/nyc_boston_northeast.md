# VC, investor and startup events for defence, dual-use and national-security founders in the US Northeast (NYC, Boston/Cambridge, Philadelphia), 9–20 Nov 2026

Research date: 28 Sep 2026.

**Method caveat (read first):** The network egress proxy blocked WebFetch and curl for every event site tried: youngstartup.com, newnorthvc.com, cfany.org, luma.com, defensetechweek.com and deep-tech-week.com all returned "EGRESS_BLOCKED" or "CONNECT 403". So every finding below comes from search-engine result snippets, not full pages. For that reason nothing is marked better than PARTLY VERIFIED unless a snippet gave the exact date and venue. Luma, Partiful, Meetup and LinkedIn listings for small meetups could not be browsed directly. That is the biggest gap in these notes.

## Q1. Which defence-tech or dual-use events run in NYC between 9 and 20 Nov 2026?

No NYC event that is specifically about defence tech could be confirmed for 9–20 Nov 2026. The most defence-heavy NYC investor events in 2026 either fell outside the window (Payload's Space & Defense Investor Summit on 3 Nov, the NY National Security Forum on 28–29 Sep, the CFA Society NY Defense Tech Investment Forum in March) or have no NYC edition that could be confirmed (a16z American Dynamism, Hill & Valley).
