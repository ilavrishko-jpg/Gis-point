## What not to write

- Do not write "we are happy to discuss this further" in place of an answer. Every question
  answered with an offer to talk is read as a question avoided.
- Do not answer "approximately" or "around" on flight-test figures. Either we have the log or
  we do not.
- Do not attach the current one-pager unchanged if its numbers contradict this response.
- Do not promise a date for the data room that we are not certain of. One missed self-imposed
  deadline in DD is expensive.
