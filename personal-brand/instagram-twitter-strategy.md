# Personal Brand Strategy — Instagram & Twitter/X

**Goal:** Present your personality and build trust with potential clients and partners in the **UK and North America**, as the founder of an international group of companies.

**Visibility level:** *Professional* — you appear on camera and lead with business, leadership, and ideas. Family stays light (values, not private details), and failures are framed as **professional lessons**, not raw personal confession.

**Positioning in one line:**
> *Global engineering entrepreneur — founder of International Engineering Group (since 2009) and COO of GIS-Point — based between the UK and Ukraine, building companies and products across borders.*

---

## 1. Is social media the best way to present your personality? (Honest answer)

Yes — but with the right channel roles. Do **not** replace LinkedIn with Instagram.

| Channel | Real role for you | Priority |
|---|---|---|
| **LinkedIn** | Where your B2B engineering clients actually decide. Primary channel for leads, credibility, deals. | ★★★★★ |
| **Instagram** | The *human + personality* layer. Makes you memorable and trusted **after** people find you. Great for storytelling, behind-the-scenes, lifestyle, values. | ★★★★☆ |
| **Twitter / X** | The *ideas + authority* layer. Fast thoughts on engineering, entrepreneurship, global business, leadership. Good for reaching Western founders and operators. | ★★★☆☆ |

**Key principle:** one personal brand, three surfaces. Same story, adapted to each platform's format.

---

## 2. The 4 Content Pillars ("tiles")

Everything you post fits into one of four buckets. This keeps your feed consistent and your personality clear. Aim for a rough balance across the four.

### Tile 1 — The Builder (Business & Engineering)
What you actually do and build.
- Behind the scenes of International Engineering Group & GIS-Point
- The two products you launched inside the group — problem, solution, impact
- Global expansion: entering UK / North America markets
- Engineering projects, sites, teams, wins
- *Purpose: credibility and "this person builds real things."*

### Tile 2 — The Entrepreneur's Lessons (Journey & Resilience)
Your most valuable and relatable asset — kept at a professional register.
- You've built startups, projects, and companies your whole life
- The startups that **didn't work** — framed as *business lessons* (market timing, focus, capital, team), not personal drama
- Why resilience and judgment, not luck, are the real entrepreneurial skills
- Decisions you'd make differently; practical lessons for founders and operators
- *Purpose: credibility and trust. Western audiences respect founders who share hard-won lessons — you can do this without over-exposing yourself.*

### Tile 3 — Mindset & Coaching (Leadership & Growth)
Where your NLP certification becomes an advantage.
- Leadership, discipline, focus, decision-making under pressure
- NLP-based frameworks for mindset and communication
- The marathon: what endurance sport teaches about business
- Habits and routines that keep a global operator sane
- *Purpose: positions you as more than a businessman — a leader people learn from.*

### Tile 4 — The Human (Values & Perspective)
The part that makes you likeable and real — kept professional.
- Values and the "why" behind the work (mention being a father of three as *motivation and perspective*, without private details or kids' faces)
- Culture, principles, how you lead and make decisions
- Travel between markets, workspaces, what a global operator's week looks like
- *Purpose: relatability. People do business with humans, not logos — but you control how much you reveal.*

## 6. Cadence & formats

- **Instagram:** 3–4 posts/week. Mix **Reels** (face-to-camera, 20–45s — best reach), **carousels** (lessons, frameworks), and photos. Use **Stories daily** — this is where the "personality" and everyday trust is built.
- **Twitter/X:** 1–2 posts/day. Short lessons, opinions, threads. Repurpose your carousel lessons into threads.
- **Repurpose once, publish three times:** one idea → Reel (IG) + thread (X) + text post (LinkedIn).
- **Face on camera** matters most — you want to "perform your personality," and people connect to a face and voice far more than to graphics.

---

## 7. What NOT to post
- Don't turn it into a company ad feed — it's *you*, not a brochure.
- Don't hide the failures — polished-only feeds read as inauthentic in UK/NA.
- Don't overshare private family details / kids' faces if you're not comfortable — show values, not everything.
- Don't chase trends that clash with your positioning (dances, unrelated memes).
- Don't buy followers — kills B2B credibility.

---

## 8. 30-day launch plan
- **Week 1:** Lock positioning, bios, handle, link page. Shoot 9 grid posts + 5 Reels in one batch.
- **Week 2:** Publish the 9-post grid. Post Stories daily. Start following/engaging with target clients & peers in UK/NA.
- **Week 3:** First "failure lesson" Reel + first X thread. Reply to comments personally.
- **Week 4:** Review what performed. Double down on the top pillar. Book a monthly batch-content shoot.

---

## 9. What "good" looks like (metrics)
Don't chase follower count. Track:
- **Saves & shares** (real value signal on IG)
- **Profile visits → link clicks** (intent)
- **DMs and meaningful comments** from your target market
- **Inbound conversations** with potential clients/partners

---

### Bottom line
Launch Instagram and Twitter — but as the **personality and trust layer** on top of LinkedIn, not a replacement. Lead with the three things only you have: **15 years building a real international engineering group, a lifelong builder who has failed and kept going, and a coach-certified leader.** Keep everything inside the four tiles, show your face, and be honest about the journey. That is what actually "performs a personality" in the modern Western market.
